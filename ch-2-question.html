🧠 Type 10 — Conceptual 2-Mark Questions ▶
  1. Define a number system. Name the four number systems commonly used in computing.
  2. Differentiate between binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal number systems on the basis of their bases and digits used.
  3. What is positional notation? Explain with one suitable example.
  4. What are MSB and LSB? Explain with an example.
  5. Explain the method used to convert a decimal integer into another number system.
  6. Explain the difference between converting a decimal integer and a decimal fraction into another number system.
  7. Why are binary digits grouped in sets of 3 when converting binary to octal?
  8. Why are binary digits grouped in sets of 4 when converting binary to hexadecimal?
  9. Write the hexadecimal equivalents of decimal numbers 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15.
  10. Write the binary equivalents of octal digits 0–7.
  11. Write the binary equivalents of hexadecimal digits 0–F.

  4. Decimal → Binary (divide by 2) and fraction (multiply by 2)
  5. Binary → Decimal (positional weights)
  6. Decimal → Octal (÷8) and Decimal → Hex (÷16)
  7. Binary ↔ Octal (3-bit grouping)
  8. Binary ↔ Hex (4-bit grouping)
  9. Integer padding → LEFT, Fraction padding → RIGHT
  10. Remainders read BOTTOM → TOP, Multiplication results read TOP → BOTTOM
